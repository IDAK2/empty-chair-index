# Design identity: civic chamber

The interface is an amphitheatre, not a dashboard. Chairs occupy a circular floor around a single podium; representation is perceived spatially before it is read numerically. Empty seats are outlined ivory forms. Filled seats receive a warm coral cushion and a testimony marker.

Typography pairs a ceremonial serif with small archival labels. The palette is aubergine, parchment, coral, and mint. The interaction moves from chamber creation to one focused podium rather than exposing a permanent wall of form controls.
